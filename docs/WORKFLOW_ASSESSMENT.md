# One workflow. A clear next decision.

The lab is useful without purchasing HALTSEAL services. A paid assessment is an optional, scoped next step when the operator wants help relating findings to an actual payment workflow.

## Bring three things

1. The workflow and payment obligation being considered.
2. The original execution path and every known replacement path, including manual portals.
3. The decision owner and any deadline for deciding what to change.

Use aliases and a redacted sample. Do not email or upload live credentials, bank account numbers, or raw personal data as part of an initial inquiry.

Bring `owner-summary.html` with its companion report and JSON when available. A synthetic example or matched fixture suite is a starting question, not evidence of an actual customer incident or complete production coverage.

## When paid review is useful

Use the free lab to fix decision code, correct record mapping, add missing records, and keep the regression checks. A scoped paid assessment is useful when one actual workflow still needs its obligation mapping, independent/manual paths, evidence authority, decision responsibility, or execution boundary examined. The paid value comes from customer-specific application and validation, not from withholding the diagnostic result or a free user's ability to fix code.

Agree whether the next decision is to fix a known gap, obtain missing evidence, retain existing controls within the reviewed scope, or evaluate a specific control boundary. A no-finding trace can still have an unverified mapping or omitted path; a fixture mismatch can be a conservative refusal rather than unsafe acceptance.

The owner summary keeps actual-workflow questions open even when synthetic expectations match. Paid help is optional; no mismatch automatically qualifies a buyer or requires a purchase.

## Agree scope before starting

Define one workflow, the evidence available, the review window, the people needed, a fixed fee, deliverables, and data-handling arrangements. This lab does not establish a price, imply a contractual engagement, or automatically transmit its report.

The assessment delivers a workflow readout, a one-page explanation of findings and unknowns, supporting structured records, and a recommended next action. Conclusions remain bounded by the evidence actually reviewed.

## Progression

Paid assessment can lead to a customer-hosted shadow evaluation with **no payment authority**. Selective enforcement requires technical validation and customer approval. Live provider writes and production claims are outside v0.2's scope.
